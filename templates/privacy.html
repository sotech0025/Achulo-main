Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2024

1. Introduction

ACHOULO ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our services.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Identification Information:
  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Date of Birth
  • Government-Issued ID Numbers (NIN, Passport, Driver's License)
  • Address and Location
Financial Information:
  • Bank Verification Number (BVN)
  • Transaction History
  • Payment Methods (securely tokenized)
Business Information (for Sellers):
  • Business Registration Number (CAC)
  • Business License
  • Property Documentation
Technical Information:
  • IP Address
  • Browser Type and Version
  • Operating System
  • Device Information
  • Usage Data and Log Files

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for:

  • Account Management: Creating and maintaining your account
  • KYC Compliance: Verifying your identity and preventing fraud
  • Transaction Processing: Facilitating secure transactions
  • Security: Detecting and preventing illegal activities
  • Communication: Sending updates, alerts, and support messages
  • Service Improvement: Analyzing usage to improve our Platform
  • Legal Compliance: Meeting regulatory and legal requirements
  • Marketing: Sending promotional content (with your consent)

4. Sharing Your Information

We may share your information with:

  • Other Users: Limited information as necessary for transactions (name, verified status)
  • Service Providers: Payment processors, verification services, hosting providers
  • Regulatory Bodies: When required by law or legal process
  • Law Enforcement: In case of fraud or security threats
  • Our Affiliates: For fraud prevention and security purposes

We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Data Security

We employ multiple security measures to protect your information:

  • AES-256 Encryption for data at rest
  • TLS 1.3 Encryption for data in transit
  • SSL Certificate (HTTPS)
  • Secure password hashing (PBKDF2-SHA256)
  • Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) available
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Compliance with PCI DSS for payment data
  • Firewalls and intrusion detection systems
  • Limited employee access to sensitive data
  • Regular data backups and disaster recovery

6. Data Retention

We retain your information:

  • Active Accounts: For the duration of your account
  • Deleted Accounts: 90 days (after which data is permanently deleted)
  • Transaction Records: 7 years (as required by law)
  • Fraud Investigation Data: 2-3 years

7. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Maintain your session
  • Remember preferences
  • Analyze Platform usage
  • Prevent fraud

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work properly.

8. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (with exceptions for legal obligations)
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Opt-Out: Decline marketing communications

To exercise these rights, email privacy@achoulo.com with your request.

9. Third-Party Links

Our Platform may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies before providing information.

10. Children's Privacy

ACHOULO is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If we discover a minor has created an account, we will delete it immediately.

11. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes are effective immediately upon posting. Your continued use of the Platform constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

For privacy concerns or data requests, contact:

  • Email: privacy@achoulo.com
  • Phone: +234 (0) 123 456 7890
  • Address: Lagos, Nigeria
  • Response Time: 10 business days

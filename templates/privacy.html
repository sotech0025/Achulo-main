Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information ACHULO collects when you use the platform, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We've tried to write it in plain language rather than dense legal text — if anything is unclear, reach out and we'll explain it.

1. Information you give us

When you register: name, email, phone number, and password.

If you register as a Listing Agent: a government-issued certificate or ID, which we use only to verify your account before you can publish listings. This document is reviewed by our admin team and is not shown publicly.

If you list a property: the listing details you provide (address, price, description, photos) and any supporting property documents you choose to upload.

When you message another user or contact support: the content of that message.

2. Information collected automatically

Like most web platforms, we automatically log basic technical information when you use ACHULO — your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit — mainly for security (e.g. detecting repeated failed logins) and to diagnose problems.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and to let you log in
  • To review and verify Listing Agent certificates and property listings
  • To connect Buyers and Listing Agents through in-app messaging
  • To process escrow payments and keep a record of transactions
  • To investigate reports of fraud, abuse, or policy violations
  • To contact you about your account — for example, if your certificate is rejected or your account is suspended

We don't sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Who can see what

Your name is visible to other users you interact with (e.g., a Buyer messaging you). A Listing Agent's verification status (verified / unverified) is shown publicly on their listings so Buyers can make an informed decision — the certificate itself is never shown publicly, only to our admin team during review. Your phone number, email, and uploaded documents are not visible to other users unless you choose to share them directly in a message.

5. How long we keep information

We keep account information for as long as your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we'll remove your personal information, though we may retain transaction records for a period where we're required to for legal or accounting purposes.

6. Your choices

  • You can update your account details at any time from your dashboard
  • You can request a copy of the information we hold about you
  • You can request that we delete your account and associated personal data
  • If your account is suspended or banned, you can submit a request for review

To exercise any of these, email privacy@achulo.com.

7. Security

Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing (never in plain text), and uploaded documents are stored on access-controlled infrastructure. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to how we handle your information, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you directly.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to privacy@achulo.com.

This document is a general template and hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer. Before you publish it, have it checked against the Nigeria Data Protection Act and any other regulation relevant to your business.

How ACHULO Keeps You Safe

An overview of the checks and safeguards behind every listing.

{{ icons.shield_check('h-5 w-5 text-primary shrink-0 mt-0.5') }}

Certificate review for Listing Agents

Before anyone can publish a listing, they submit a government certificate or ID. An admin reviews it before the account is marked verified.

{{ icons.clock('h-5 w-5 text-primary shrink-0 mt-0.5') }}

Listings are reviewed before going live

A new listing isn't visible to Buyers until an admin has approved it, not just when it's submitted.

{{ icons.lock('h-5 w-5 text-primary shrink-0 mt-0.5') }}

Escrow-held payments

Funds you commit to a property stay in escrow rather than going straight to the Listing Agent, until you've confirmed the property is as described.

{{ icons.check_circle('h-5 w-5 text-primary shrink-0 mt-0.5') }}

Human-reviewed reports

Reporting a listing or user sends it to an admin for review — we don't auto-remove anything based on report volume alone.

Account security

Passwords are hashed before storage — we never store or have access to your plain-text password. If your account shows signs of suspicious activity (repeated failed logins, unfamiliar access), we may temporarily lock it as a precaution.

Suspensions, bans & appeals

If an account or listing is found to violate our Terms of Service, an admin may suspend it (temporary restriction) or ban it (blocked from logging in). If you believe an action against your account was a mistake, you can submit a request for review and an admin will look at it personally.

Reporting something suspicious

You can report any listing directly from its page. Include as much detail as you can — this goes straight to our admin team for review, not an automated filter.

What we can't promise

Verification reduces risk, but it isn't a legal guarantee of title or ownership. Buyers should still take reasonable steps of their own — visiting the property, asking questions directly, and reviewing paperwork — before making a payment.

Contact

To report a security concern, email security@achulo.com. See also our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

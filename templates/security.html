How to Protect Your Account

1. Create a Strong Password
  • Use at least 12 characters (we require 8)
  • Mix uppercase and lowercase letters
  • Include numbers and special characters
  • Never use personal information (names, birthdates)
  • Avoid dictionary words
  • Use unique passwords for each account
2. Enable Two-Factor Authentication
  • Access Settings → Security
  • Enable 2FA with authenticator app or SMS
  • Save backup codes in a safe place
  • Update your recovery email
3. Secure Your Device
  • Keep OS and antivirus software updated
  • Use a firewall
  • Don't use public WiFi for sensitive transactions
  • Use VPN on public networks
  • Lock your device with password/biometrics
4. Monitor Your Account
  • Review login history regularly
  • Check active sessions
  • Report suspicious activity immediately
  • Update your phone number if changed

Fraud Prevention & Warning Signs

⚠️ Common Scams to Avoid:
  • Overpayment Scams: Fake checks or advance payments for properties
  • Phishing: Emails asking you to "verify" your account or update payment info
  • Fake Listings: Properties listed multiple times with different prices
  • Impersonation: Fake agents claiming to represent ACHOULO
  • Wire Transfer Fraud: Requests to wire money outside the Platform
  • Fake Documentation: Forged property deeds or ownership certificates
  • Rental Scams: Properties rented at prices too good to be true
Red Flags:
  • Pressure to move conversations off-platform (to WhatsApp, email)
  • Requests for upfront payments or deposits
  • Unwillingness to verify identity or documentation
  • Poor grammar or spelling in communications
  • Prices significantly below market value
  • Agents who cannot provide valid credentials
  • Requests to pay via cryptocurrency or gift cards
  • Urgency to make quick decisions

Document Safety

When Uploading Documents:
  • Only upload to ACHOULO Platform (never share via email/WhatsApp)
  • Verify you're on the official website (check SSL certificate)
  • Use secure internet connection
  • Don't upload bank account details or full card numbers
  • Redact sensitive information if not required
Document Verification Process:
  • ACHOULO will never ask for sensitive docs via email
  • All uploads are encrypted and securely stored
  • Only authorized admin staff can access documents
  • Documents are verified within 24-48 hours
  • You'll receive notification once verified

Reporting Suspicious Activity

If you encounter fraud or suspicious activity:
  1. Don't engage further with the suspicious user
  2. Screenshot evidence of the suspicious activity
  3. Report immediately using our report system
  4. Document details: Date, time, user information, messages
  5. Contact support: security@achoulo.com
  6. If financial loss: Report to CBN and local police

What ACHOULO Never Does

  • Ask for your password via email or phone
  • Process payments outside the Platform
  • Ask for banking credentials or card details
  • Request personal documents via email
  • Guarantee property deals or outcomes
  • Ask you to pay fees upfront (except verified transactions)
  • Contact you with unsolicited investment offers

For Sellers/Agents

  • Verify buyer credentials before engagement
  • Never accept payment outside the Platform
  • Upload authentic property documentation
  • Use secure communication methods
  • Keep login credentials private
  • Report suspicious buyer inquiries
  • Maintain updated insurance and licenses
  • Follow all regulatory requirements

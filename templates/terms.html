Last updated: August 2026

These Terms of Service ("Terms") govern your access to and use of ACHULO, a platform that connects people looking to rent or buy property ("Buyers") with individuals and agents listing property ("Listing Agents"). By creating an account or using ACHULO in any way, you agree to these Terms. If you don't agree, please don't use the platform.

1. Who can use ACHULO

You must be at least 18 years old and able to form a binding contract under Nigerian law to create an account. When you register, the information you provide — your name, contact details, and, for Listing Agents, your government certificate/ID — must be accurate and belong to you. Accounts created with false information may be suspended or banned.

2. Two kinds of accounts

Buyers can browse listings, message Listing Agents, and place funds in escrow toward a property. Buyers are responsible for reviewing a listing carefully and asking questions before committing to a payment.

Listing Agents can publish, edit, and manage property listings. Before a Listing Agent account is trusted on the platform, we require a government-issued certificate or ID, which our team reviews. A Listing Agent is responsible for making sure every listing they publish is accurate, that they have the right to list the property, and that photos and descriptions aren't misleading.

3. Verification & admin review

We review the certificate submitted at registration before a Listing Agent's account is marked verified, and we review individual listings before they go live. Verification reduces risk but is not a guarantee — it does not certify legal title to a property, and Buyers should still take reasonable steps (site visits, independent checks) before making a payment.

4. Escrow payments

When a Buyer pays through ACHULO, funds are held in escrow rather than sent directly to the Listing Agent. The Buyer is expected to confirm the property matches what was listed before funds are released. We reserve the right to pause a release or investigate a transaction if either party reports a problem.

5. Messaging

Buyers and Listing Agents can message each other about a specific listing through the platform. Please keep communication respectful and on-topic. Don't use messaging to send spam, harassment, or content unrelated to a property inquiry.

6. What's not allowed

  • Listing a property you don't have the right to list, or that doesn't exist
  • Uploading a certificate, ID, or document that isn't genuinely yours
  • Misrepresenting a property's condition, price, or availability
  • Harassing, threatening, or attempting to defraud another user
  • Creating a second account to get around a suspension or ban
  • Scraping, reverse-engineering, or interfering with the platform's normal operation

7. Reports, suspension & bans

Any user can report a listing or account they believe is fraudulent or abusive. Our admin team reviews reports individually — we don't take listings down or restrict accounts automatically. Depending on what we find, an account may be suspended (temporarily restricted) or banned (blocked from logging in). A banned user can submit a request for review, which an admin will personally consider.
